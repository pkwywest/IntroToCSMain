# Copy the Course

1. First, click on the course title from your Home page. This will open the content.

    ![Step 1: Click on the course title](/api/files/5f2c70b347d1fc1c1a4dcbd2/open-the-course.png "Open the course.png")

1. Once inside, click on the *Course* dropdown button in the upper right corner of the screen. Select the *Make a Copy* option.

    ![Course-menu-option.png](/api/files/5f2c714a47d1fc1c1a4dcd72/course-menu-option.png "Course-menu-option.png")

1. Return to your Home screen. You will see that a new course has been added, and that you are listed as the *Owner*. Click on the new title to access the content.

    ![course-owner.png](/api/files/5f2c72a847d1fc1c1a4dd146/course-owner.png "course-owner.png")

1. Once inside, select *Edit Course Details* from the *Course* button.

    ![edit-details.png](/api/files/5f2c733147d1fc1c1a4dd2ff/edit-details.png "edit-details.png")

1. Rename your course to suit the class you are going to teach!

## Edit to Your Heart's Content!

With your paid Trinket account, you can personalize the layout, add new pages or assignments, and manage the class.

Of course, don't forget to invite your students!

Any changes you make will NOT affect the original.

## Keep Your Own Master Copy

Once you have personalized your new class, you can make copies of it just like before. This is a good idea if you expect to teach multile sections of LCHS.

Note that copying a course only moves *content* and does NOT inlcude enrolled students.

Also, updates made to the master will not appear in the clones.
